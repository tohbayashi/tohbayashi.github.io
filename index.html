02 / RESEARCH

Research

Sensory Integration

How different sensory modalities are integrated within the nervous system to generate appropriate behavioral responses.

Calcium Dynamics

Investigating intracellular calcium dynamics and their relationship to neural activity.

Neural Circuits

Understanding the neural circuits that connect sensory information to behavioral outputs.

Protein–Protein Interaction

Investigation of ligand–GPCR interactions in the osmosensory pathway using Co-immunoprecipitation (Co-IP) and Blue Native Polyacrylamide Gel Electrophoresis (BN-PAGE)
